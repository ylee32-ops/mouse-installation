Preview

Solo plays one character's sequences end to end on a loop, so you can judge its speed and framing without waiting out the quiet timer.

Photos

Where build_scenes.py looks for the full-resolution photos. Load frames_preview/ here in the browser, but this should point at the drive holding frames/. Import your existing scenes.json and it fills in by itself.

Screen

Your photos are 3:2 and most screens are 16:9. Flip this while a sequence plays to see exactly what each costs — then set the same value in scenes.json and re-run build_scenes.py. Nothing is baked in until you encode, so this can wait until the very end.

Timing

Unbroken quiet before a mouse comes out (0 → 1).

Dead time on the base image after a retreat.

finish_fast plays out the rest of the current loop cycle at this speed, so the hand-off to 3 lands on the pose the loop was shot to end on. cut is more startling but can jump the pose.

Speed of the rewind when sound interrupts the out sequence.

Easter eggs

A dog takes the slot a mouse would have used, this many emergences apart (random within the range).

A room reacting to the dog would otherwise have to fall silent for the full quiet_seconds all over again. This shortens the wait just after an easter egg.

Sound

Tracks the room's own noise floor so a hum doesn't keep the mouse hidden.
